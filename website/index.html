Reach your users everywhere, from your own server.

Carillon sends push notifications, emails, SMS, WhatsApp and in-app messages to the users of all your apps. No subscriber limit, no bill per message — and it speaks the OneSignal API.

Everything a growth team needs

The features you pay for elsewhere, on a server you control.

Every channel

Push to iOS, Android, Huawei, Amazon and the web; emails with a visual editor; SMS with Twilio or Orange; WhatsApp templates.

Journeys

Welcome series, win-back, abandoned cart: triggers, waits, conditions, branches and sending hours.

Smart send time

Each person gets the message at the hour they usually open the app — or at the same local time for everyone.

Segments

Tags, sessions, countries, app versions, events, money spent, last click. Live counts while you build them.

In-app messages

Banners, pop-ups and full screens with recipes, delays, frequency rules and A/B tests.

Insights

From delivery to conversion, retention by cohort, who is online now, A/B results and a weekly email report.

Leave OneSignal in an afternoon

Same API, same SDK calls. Import your subscribers and keep reaching them while your apps update.

  • Same REST API — POST /api/v1/notifications, segments, filters, external ids, templates.
  • Nightly sync of your OneSignal app: devices of the old app version keep receiving.
  • SDKs with the OneSignal API for Flutter and the web.
  • Your keys: Apple, Firebase, Huawei, Amazon — no middleman.

Built to be run by you

  • One Docker image, SQLite or PostgreSQL, ready for Coolify.
  • Configured from the browser: keys, mail, security. Secrets encrypted.
  • Private by design: invitations only, 2FA, single sign-on, audit log.
  • Health page for the queue, the scheduler and the disk.
